	EN	[Early Alpha / Work in Progress] Experimental port of ZXTune (r5112) for Daphile / Lyrion Music Server (LMS): decodes chiptune, tracker, and videogame music formats, unpacks multi-track archives, streams Random Radio feeds, and browses online chiptune/VGM catalogues (ZXTunes, ZXArt, ModArchive, ModLand, HVSC, ASMA, AYGOR, JoshW, AMP, Aminet, Scene.org, VGMRips, Khinsider, OCReMix). Some features and online catalogues are still under active development and testing.

Alcune funzioni e cataloghi sono ancora in fase di sviluppo e test.
